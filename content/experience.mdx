During my career I had the chance to work on many different projects — from small personal projects to large corporate applications serving thousands of customers.

As all projects were unique, I encountered many different challenges, but I was always able to find a solution or negotiate a good compromise to satisfy all interested parties.

## Uniqa.at *(OCT 2020 – Present)*

At Uniqa.at I had a chance to work on their customer portal MyUniqa. Later I was transferred to work on transforming their internal applications. I work in a frontend team of 3 FE developers, where I am the lead developer. Our goal is to introduce new sustainable technology and improve UX and UI.

## Revolt.city *(DEC 2020 – JUL 2021)*

For Revolt.city I had a chance to develop a mobile application for their servicemen. It was built using the latest trends in hybrid development — Angular, Ionic 5 and Capacitor.

Usage of those technologies allowed fast delivery and reduced cost of development and maintenance compared to native counterparts.

## USU Software s.r.o. *(JUL 2017 – JUL 2020)*

USU is a large corporation that develops many different products and solutions. I had a chance to work on a BI tool that helped improve ETL processes, built with Angular using Angular Material components.

Later, when the company was consolidating their different sister companies under one brand, I initiated a movement to introduce WebComponents to unify the UI. I became lead developer for the Design System. We chose Stencil.js as the technology for building those WebComponents and it was a joy to work with.

## FlowUp s.r.o. *(JUL 2019 – JUN 2020)*

FlowUp focused on developing cloud-based applications as a Google Partner. They embraced developer communities and organised regular public meetups — mostly focused on Angular — and I'm happy to have been part of that. I attended two Firebase conferences, one in Prague and one in Madrid.

The project I worked on was UK-based fintech startup Dashly, which aims to give people a better understanding of their mortgage or help find a better deal. I was mostly responsible for removing technical debt and developing new features. I gained a lot of practical knowledge of Firebase-related tools.

## Pindex.sk *(MAR 2014 – JUL 2016)*

Pindex.sk is a professional network for students and teachers of universities and high schools in Slovakia, where students could share notes about teachers and grade them based on objective metrics. It was an interesting experience — especially because at the time I was a student myself.

I rewrote their platform to new standards using an up-to-date version of Laravel with performance in mind. It was a wonderful experience because I had to implement a pixel-perfect design given by a very skilled designer.
